# Prismatic Stone

`Prismatic Stone` is a luminous decorative stone found in `Crystal Caverns`.

## Where It Appears

- Generated in clusters inside `Crystal Caverns`
- Often mixed into marble, calcite, and basalt-heavy cave sections

## Uses

- Decorative building block
- Full stone family:
  - `Prismatic Stone`
  - `Polished Prismatic Stone`
  - `Prismatic Bricks`
  - `Prismatic Tiles`
  - stairs, slabs, and walls

## Notes

- Emits a faint glow
- Intended as the signature building stone of `Crystal Caverns`
