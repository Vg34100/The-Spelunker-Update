# Electrum

## Overview

Electrum is a late alloy made from silver and gold in the foundry.

It is meant to support premium enchantment-adjacent gear and fast utility equipment rather than basic progression.

## Production

- Produced in a `Foundry` placed directly above a filled `Crucible`
- Current recipe:
  - `1 Gold Ingot`
  - `1 Silver Ingot`
  - consumes `1` crucible lava charge
  - yields `2 Electrum Ingots`

## Storage

- `Electrum Ingots` can be crafted into a `Block of Electrum`
- `Electrum Block` can be crafted back into `9 Electrum Ingots`
- `Electrum Tiles` are available as a decorative block

## Current Uses

- `Electrum Bow` draws faster than a normal bow
- Electrum blocks and tiles provide bright decorative metal building options

## Planned Uses

- Enchant-focused equipment
- More specialized utility gear
