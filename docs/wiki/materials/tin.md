# Tin

## Overview

Tin is an early cave utility metal found in the overworld.

It is intended to support expedition gear and later alloying without being useful only as an alloy ingredient.

## Generation

- Generates in the overworld.
- Appears as `Tin Ore` in stone and `Deepslate Tin Ore` in deepslate.
- Generation range:
  - minimum: `y=-24`
  - maximum: `y=72`
  - most common around: `y=24`
- Vein size: up to `8`
- Placement attempts: `14` per chunk

## Drops

- Breaking tin ore normally drops `Raw Tin`.
- Silk Touch drops the ore block itself.
- Fortune increases raw tin drops.

## Processing

- `Raw Tin` can be smelted or blasted into `Tin Ingot`.
- `Raw Tin` can be crafted into a `Block of Raw Tin`.
- `Tin Ingots` can be crafted into a `Block of Tin`.

## Planned Uses

- Expedition and cave utility crafting
- Hardware for traversal and exploration items
- Later alloying input for bronze
