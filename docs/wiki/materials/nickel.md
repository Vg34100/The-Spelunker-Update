# Nickel

## Overview

Nickel is a deeper, rarer overworld metal focused on reinforcement, plating, and specialized crafting.

## Generation

- Generates in the overworld.
- Appears as `Nickel Ore` in stone and `Deepslate Nickel Ore` in deepslate.
- Generation range:
  - minimum: `y=-48`
  - maximum: `y=20`
  - most common around: `y=-14`
- Vein size: up to `6`
- Placement attempts: `7` per chunk

## Drops

- Breaking nickel ore normally drops `Raw Nickel`.
- Silk Touch drops the ore block itself.
- Fortune increases raw nickel drops.

## Processing

- `Raw Nickel` can be smelted or blasted into `Nickel Ingot`.
- `Raw Nickel` can be crafted into a `Block of Raw Nickel`.
- `Nickel Ingots` can be crafted into a `Block of Nickel`.

## Planned Uses

- Reinforcement and plating systems
- Later alloying input for invar
- Durable expedition hardware
