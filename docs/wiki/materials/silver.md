# Silver

## Overview

Silver is an overworld precious metal with combat and utility uses tied to undead, potions, and later alloying.

## Generation

- Generates in the overworld.
- Appears as `Silver Ore` in stone and `Deepslate Silver Ore` in deepslate.
- Generation range:
  - minimum: `y=-32`
  - maximum: `y=48`
  - most common around: `y=8`
- Vein size: up to `7`
- Placement attempts: `9` per chunk

## Drops

- Breaking silver ore normally drops `Raw Silver`.
- Silk Touch drops the ore block itself.
- Fortune increases raw silver drops.

## Processing

- `Raw Silver` can be smelted or blasted into `Silver Ingot`.
- `Raw Silver` can be crafted into a `Block of Raw Silver`.
- `Silver Ingots` can be crafted into a `Block of Silver`.

## Planned Uses

- Weapons that excel against undead
- Silver sword
- Silver arrows
- Silver lining with wither immunity
- Later alloying input for electrum

## Current Uses

- `Silver Sword` deals bonus damage to undead
- `Silver Arrow` deals bonus damage to undead
- `Silver Lining` upgrades iron armor into `Silver-Lined Iron Armor`
- Any silver-lined armor piece grants full wither immunity while worn

## Gear Direction

- Silver is intended to support an undead-focused tool and armor set
- The set should stay specialized rather than becoming a universal combat upgrade
