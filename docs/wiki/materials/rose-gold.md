# Rose Gold

## Overview

Rose gold is an alloy built around cave instruments and enchantment-adjacent utility rather than raw combat power.

## Production

- Produced in a `Foundry` placed directly above a filled `Crucible`
- Current recipe:
  - `1 Gold Ingot`
  - `1 Copper Ingot`
  - consumes `1` crucible lava charge
  - yields `2 Rose Gold Ingots`

## Storage

- `Rose Gold Ingots` can be crafted into a `Block of Rose Gold`
- `Rose Gold Block` can be crafted back into `9 Rose Gold Ingots`
- `Rose Gold Tiles` are available as a decorative block

## Current Uses

- `Prospector's Lens` scans nearby stone for ore signatures

## Planned Uses

- More cave information tools
- Enchantability-focused equipment
- Rose gold plating
