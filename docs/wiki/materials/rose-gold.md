# Rose Gold

## Overview

Rose gold is an alloy built around cave instruments and enchantment-adjacent utility rather than raw combat power.

## Production

- Produced in a `Foundry` placed directly above a filled `Crucible`
- Current recipe:
  - `2 Gold Ingots`
  - `1 Copper Ingot`
  - consumes `1` crucible lava charge
  - yields `3 Rose Gold Ingots`

## Storage

- `Rose Gold Ingots` can be crafted into a `Block of Rose Gold`
- `Rose Gold Block` can be crafted back into `9 Rose Gold Ingots`
- `Rose Gold Tiles` are available as a decorative block

## Current Uses

- `Prospector's Lens` scans nearby stone for ore signatures
- `Rose Gold Filigree` upgrades iron armor into `Rose Gold-Plated Iron Armor`
- `Rose Gold Filigree` crafting recipe:
  - ` m `
  - `mpm`
  - ` m `
  - `m = Rose Gold Ingot`
  - `p = Paper`
- Rose gold-plated armor is the first enchantability-focused armor upgrade path

## Gear Direction

- Rose gold is intended to support a luckier, more enchantment-friendly tool and armor set
- The set should lean into refinement and upgrade quality rather than raw toughness

## Planned Uses

- More cave information tools
- Enchantability-focused equipment
- Rose gold plating
