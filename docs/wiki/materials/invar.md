# Invar

## Overview

Invar is a nickel-and-iron alloy built around durability, stability, and premium workstations.

## Production

- Produced in a `Foundry` placed directly above a filled `Crucible`
- Current recipe:
  - `2 Iron Ingots`
  - `1 Nickel Ingot`
  - consumes `1` crucible lava charge
  - yields `2 Invar Ingots`

## Storage

- `Invar Ingots` can be crafted into a `Block of Invar`
- `Invar Block` can be crafted back into `9 Invar Ingots`
- `Invar Tiles` are available as a decorative block

## Current Uses

- `Invar Anvil` now uses its own slower-degrading damage chain
- `Chipped Invar Anvil` and `Damaged Invar Anvil` are intermediate wear states
- Invar blocks and tiles provide durable industrial building material

## Notes

- Invar anvils degrade much more slowly than vanilla anvils
- They still eventually wear out under heavy use
