# Expedition Gear

## Overview

These are the newer cave-focused utility additions planned for Spelunkery.

They are meant to support cave travel, scouting, and emergency escape rather than raw combat power.

## Torch Launcher

- A ranged torch-placement tool
- Intended for awkward ledges, shafts, and dark ceilings
- Best used as a convenience item rather than a combat weapon

## Rope Bundle

- A compact rope crafting item
- Intended to support deployable rope columns
- This is the material component for the rope system rather than the final traversal block itself

## Rope

- A climbable traversal block
- Intended to behave like a cave rope column rather than ordinary building scaffolding
- Best used for shafts, drop routes, and quick vertical travel

## Bat Wing

- Rare bat drop
- Intended as an alchemy component for cave-focused tonics
- Used as a low-tier organic reagent for underground utility crafting

## Spelunker's Brew

- Cave scouting drink
- Intended to provide constant ore awareness rather than a short pulse
- Best used when actively exploring new tunnels and cave walls

## Dangersense Tonic

- Cave safety drink
- Intended to provide constant mob awareness rather than a short pulse
- Best used when moving through dark or crowded cave systems

## Miner's Tonic

- Mining support drink
- Intended to boost mining speed on stone and ore blocks
- Best used for short excavation bursts, not as an always-on replacement for tools
