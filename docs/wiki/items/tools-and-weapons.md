# Tools and Weapons

## Bronze Compass

- Crafted from `Bronze Ingots` and a normal `Compass`
- Use it to read your current `Y` level
- Intended as the first bronze cave survey tool

## Silver Sword

- Crafted from `Silver Ingots`
- Deals bonus damage to undead
- Intended as silver's first dedicated combat niche

## Silver Arrow

- Crafted from `Silver Ingot`, `Stick`, and `Feather`
- Deals bonus damage to undead targets on hit
- Works like a normal arrow outside that niche

## Electrum Bow

- Crafted from `Electrum Ingots` and `String`
- Draws faster than a normal bow
- Intended as electrum's first specialized ranged weapon

## Prospector's Lens

- Crafted from `Rose Gold Ingots`, a `Spyglass`, and `Glass Pane`
- Scans nearby blocks for ore and reports the nearest reading
- Best used while moving through fresh cave walls rather than open caverns
