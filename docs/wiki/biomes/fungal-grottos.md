# Fungal Grottos

`Fungal Grottos` are lush overworld cave biomes focused on fungal ground cover, glowcaps, glow lichen, hanging growth, and giant mushrooms.

## Identity

- Main ground blocks: `Mycelium Mud` and `Bioluminescent Moss`
- Accent growth: `Fungal Mat`, `Glowcap`, vines, glow lichen, and giant mushrooms
- Overall direction: dense underground mushroom caverns rather than lush-cave berry growth

## Resources

- `Mycelium Mud`
- `Bioluminescent Moss`
- `Glowcap`
- Giant red, brown, and blue mushroom forms

## Features

- Custom fungal overgrowth clusters
- Small brown and red mushrooms
- Giant brown, red, and blue mushrooms
- Lush cave-surface takeover without relying on vanilla glow berries

## Worldgen Note

- This biome is part of the overworld cave-biome selection for new worlds
- Existing worlds will not retroactively regenerate with it
