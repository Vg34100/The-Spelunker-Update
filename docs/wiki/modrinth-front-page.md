# The Spelunker Update

`The Spelunker Update` expands Minecraft's underground with new cave biomes, metals, alloys, gems, traversal tools, utility gear, and cave-focused crafting systems. Explore fungal grottos, magma vaults, marble caves, and crystal caverns while building new loadouts around foundry alloys, socketed miner's helmets, tonics, rope, and deeper cave resources.

## Overview

`The Spelunker Update` is a cave-expansion mod built around the idea that the underground should feel like a real progression path instead of just a place to grab ore and leave.

It adds new cave biomes, new metals and gems, new crafting systems, new utility equipment, and a stronger sense that different underground regions have their own identity. Instead of caves being mostly the same stone with different ores mixed in, this mod aims to make exploration itself feel rewarding.

## Main Features

### New Cave Biomes

The underground now includes several distinct biome types with their own terrain, mood, and resources.

- `Crystal Caverns` are bright, prismatic cave systems filled with marble, crystal spikes, and glowing stone.
- `Marble Caves` are large marble-heavy cavern regions that feel cleaner and brighter than normal underground stone.
- `Magma Vaults` are deep volcanic chambers full of cinder rock, ash, scorched dripstone, and lava-lit ruins.
- `Fungal Grottos` are giant mushroom caverns with mycelium mud, bioluminescent moss, glowcaps, fungal overgrowth, and huge mushrooms.

### New Metals, Gems, and Materials

The mod adds new overworld metals and underground gem materials that support both progression and utility.

- `Tin`, `Nickel`, and `Silver` generate as new ore lines in the overworld.
- `Topaz`, `Ruby`, and `Sapphire` appear through special cave features rather than simple ore veins.
- `Marble`, `Prismatic Stone`, `Cinder Rock`, and other biome stones provide large new building palettes.

### Foundry and Crucible Alloying

Alloys are produced through a two-block structure:

- `Crucible` stores lava as fuel
- `Foundry` processes alloy recipes above it

Current alloys include:

- `Bronze`
- `Electrum`
- `Invar`
- `Rose Gold`

This system is meant to give underground materials a stronger crafting identity than just "smelt ore into ingot."

### Cave Utility Gear

A large part of the mod is focused on gear that helps with exploration rather than only combat.

Examples include:

- `Bronze Compass` for depth reading
- `Miner's Helmet` with socketed gem effects
- `Prospector's Lens` for ore scanning
- `Torch Launcher` for ranged torch placement
- `Rope` and `Rope Bundles` for vertical cave travel
- `Bronze Shield` and `Electrum Bow` for more specialized equipment

### Armor Upgrades

Iron armor can be upgraded through smithing paths with different roles:

- `Nickel Plating` for durability and toughness
- `Silver Lining` for wither protection
- `Rose Gold Filigree` for a more arcane, luck-focused route

### Tonics and Exploration Effects

Several cave-focused consumables give short-term exploration advantages.

- `Spelunker's Brew`
- `Dangersense Tonic`
- `Miner's Tonic`

These are meant to support scouting, mining, and cave survival rather than replace normal progression.

## Design Goals

The mod is built around a few core ideas:

- caves should have regional identity
- underground exploration should have its own progression
- new materials should have distinct uses, not just higher stats
- cave tools and utility gear should matter
- building underground should be visually rewarding

## Best Fit

`The Spelunker Update` fits best in modpacks or playthroughs that want:

- more interesting cave exploration
- new building materials
- underground-focused progression
- utility-based gear instead of only combat scaling
- a stronger update-sized expansion to vanilla caves

## Notes

- Best experienced on a fresh world so the new cave biomes and underground features generate naturally.
- The mod is designed around the overworld underground rather than replacing vanilla progression entirely.
