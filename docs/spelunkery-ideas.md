# Spelunkery Update Ideas

## Overview

The Spelunkery update expands cave exploration with new gems, new ores, alloy progression, expedition gear, cave utility items, and later new cave biomes.

The focus is on making caves more rewarding, more readable, and more mechanically distinct without turning the mod into a heavy tech mod.

## Feature A: Gems and Geodes

### Amethyst

- Keep vanilla amethyst geodes as they are.
- Expand amethyst later only through new uses, not through normal ore generation.

### Emerald

- Do not add emerald geodes.
- Leave emerald mostly vanilla for now.

### Topaz

- Add topaz as a geode or crystal pocket style gem feature.
- Topaz is the clearest first new gem feature because it maps well onto the existing geode idea.

### Ruby

- Add ruby as its own gem item with its own texture and uses.
- Ruby should generate through crystal pockets rather than full amethyst-style geodes.

### Sapphire

- Add sapphire as its own gem item with its own texture and uses.
- Sapphire should generate through crystal pockets rather than full amethyst-style geodes.

### Gem Notes

- Ruby and sapphire are separate items and separate materials to the player.
- Topaz should feel more geode-like.
- Ruby and sapphire should feel rarer and more special than simple ore replacements.

## Feature B: More Ores

### Tin

- Tin is an overworld ore with normal and deepslate variants.
- Tin should be a utility metal first.
- Tin can be part of alloying later, but alloying should not be its only value.

### Nickel

- Nickel should be rarer and deeper than tin.
- Nickel is meant for reinforcement, plating, and specialized crafting.

### Silver

- Silver should be added before electrum.
- Silver weapons deal double damage to undead by default.
- Silver arrows should be added.
- Silver lining or plating grants full wither immunity.

## Feature C: Alloying

### Alloying Structure

- Alloying should use a two-block setup.
- Top block: `Foundry`
- Bottom block: `Crucible`
- The foundry only works when placed above a valid crucible.
- Lava is consumed during alloying.
- Alloying should use ingots, not raw ore.

### Bronze

- Inputs: copper and tin
- Bronze should be a utility alloy, not just a basic metal tier.
- Planned bronze uses:
  - bronze shield
  - bronze compass and cave instruments
  - cave hardware and utility items

### Bronze Shield

- The bronze shield should have a bash effect.
- The bash should trigger when blocking an incoming hit while moving into the attacker.
- The effect should be controlled and hard to trigger accidentally.

### Bronze Instruments

- Bronze should enable cave information items rather than only armor and tools.
- Candidate ideas:
  - depth meter
  - bronze compass variant
  - surveyor or cave-reading tools

### Invar

- Inputs: iron and nickel
- Invar should use its low thermal expansion identity as its theme.
- Planned invar uses:
  - invar armor with a stability or heat-focused niche
  - invar anvil
  - durable utility parts

### Invar Anvil

- The invar anvil should degrade much more slowly than a normal anvil.
- It should be a premium alloy workstation because of its cost.
- It can later gain a second enchanting or repair-related property if needed.

### Rose Gold

- Rose gold should keep the enchantability identity of gold while improving durability somewhat.
- Rose gold tools and armor should be easier to enchant and tend to receive better enchantments on average.
- Rose gold should also support specialized cave information tools.

### Rose Gold Plating

- Rose gold plating should be applied through smithing.
- It should add some enchantability to armor without being as strong as full rose gold gear.

### Rose Gold Devices

- Rose gold is a strong material for information accessories inspired by cave exploration tools.
- Candidate items:
  - depth meter
  - creature detector
  - ore or prospecting tool
  - survey kit or cave info item

### Electrum

- Electrum is the final planned alloy in this chain.
- Silver must exist before electrum.
- Electrum should have the highest-quality enchanting niche.
- Electrum should be strongly biased toward rarer or more premium enchanting outcomes, but not literally treasure-only.
- Electrum should have slightly lower raw enchantability than the most extreme version of the idea to keep it balanced.

### Electrum Bow

- The electrum bow should draw 25% faster than a normal bow.
- This is meant to be a clear special-purpose item, not just a flat upgrade to all ranged gear.

## Feature D: Gear and Utility

### Miner's Helmet

- The miner's helmet occupies the helmet slot.
- It should be weak as armor but useful enough that there is a real reason to wear it.
- The helmet uses one socketed gem at a time.
- Gem effects should be activated rather than always passive.
- Activation should consume durability over time.

### Helmet Gem Ideas

- Sapphire: strong cave vision utility
- Ruby: fire or lava protection utility
- Topaz: cave sensing, mining, or navigation utility
- Amethyst: later sensing or resonance utility

### Torch Launcher

- Add a torch launcher or throwable torch utility item.
- It should place light at distance and make hard-to-reach cave spaces easier to handle.

### Spelunker's Rope

- Add a deployable rope bundle that creates a climbable rope column down a shaft.
- It should be a traversal tool for caves and pits.
- Rope recoverability can be tuned later based on crafting cost and progression.

### Spelunker's Brew

- Add a potion or brew that gives a subtle nearby ore reveal.
- The effect should feel more like a spelunker sense than full x-ray vision.

### Miner's Potion

- Add a craftable mining-focused potion.
- It should act like a controlled haste-style effect for cave mining.

## Feature E: New Cave Biomes

### Crystal Caverns

- Crystal Caverns should be the first major cave biome addition.
- Intended palette:
  - calcite
  - smooth basalt
  - water pools
  - crystal or prismatic decorative blocks
- Gem crystal pocket generation can be elevated here.

### Magma Vaults

- Magma Vaults should be a hot overworld cave biome, not just a Nether copy.
- Candidate materials:
  - scorched dripstone
  - cinder rock
  - magma-heavy stone features
- Lava pools and heat hazards should define the space.

### Fungal Grottos

- Fungal Grottos should be damp, overgrown, and bioluminescent.
- Candidate materials:
  - mycelium mud
  - moss and rooted dirt
  - glow lichen and vines
  - spore bloom
  - bioluminescent moss

## Material Identity Summary

- Tin: utility metal
- Nickel: reinforcement metal
- Silver: undead and anti-wither metal
- Bronze: expedition utility alloy
- Invar: stable heat-resistant alloy
- Rose Gold: enchantability and information alloy
- Electrum: premium enchant-focused alloy

## Notes

- Tools and armor can exist for many metals and alloys, but they should never be the only reason the material exists.
- A full armor set should only matter if it has a clear specialized identity rather than simply trying to compete with netherite.
